Candidates Replicator

Minimum card level required for replication (1–6). Cards below this level will be skipped.

Decks Replicator

Minimum level for cards from sets 5000, 5200 and 5100. Cards from other sets must be level 6 or higher.

If enabled, any rune is accepted when the reference card has a rune.
